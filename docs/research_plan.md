# Research plan and progress

## Main question

How successful is Singapore's development model, what are its tradeoffs, and can elements of it explain development outcomes elsewhere?

Keep four questions separate: absolute outcomes, relative peer performance, institutional associations, and transferability. A regression association cannot settle transferability.

## Scope

The 11 cases are Singapore, Malaysia, Philippines, Thailand, Indonesia, South Korea, Hong Kong, Ghana, Zambia, Argentina and El Salvador. Hong Kong is a separately reported economy. Singapore's independent-state history begins in 1965. The broader international reference is not limited to these deliberately chosen cases.

Historical World Bank and V-Dem values are retained where available from 1965. Modern annual models use 2000-2024. Happiness uses explicitly identified reporting periods and a separate non-overlapping-window analysis. Do not force every source into a 1965-present annual panel.

## Completed foundations

- R collection and transformation code; original provider files retained.
- World Bank source inventory, indicator metadata and coverage audit.
- Audited V-Dem v16 identifiers and provider uncertainty.
- Published happiness-period data with explicit names and reporting windows.
- Annual global references, fixed-2010 sensitivity and clearly labeled current-high-income means.
- Provisional economic, living-standard and descriptive freedom baskets; separate happiness measure and social indicators.
- Expanded association models with control, openness, sample and basket sensitivity.
- Calendar-year tables and a qualified exploratory development-stage design.
- Plain-language datasets, source notes, plot captions and browser overview.
- Validation against original provider values and model time-alignment rules.

## Still unresolved

1. State intervention: public investment is now audited and modeled as a narrow fiscal measure. OECD ownership assessments remain periodic and incomplete; broader ownership, directed-credit and industrial-policy measurement is still unresolved.
2. Social outcomes: provider welfare, coverage and comparability-spell metadata are now audited. Separate income/consumption within-block models are available. A universal composite and unrestricted cross-survey pooling remain unsupported.
3. Happiness: a directly verified annual Gallup series remains outside the public workbook used here. The available multi-year scores are useful but different.
4. Peer groups: historical-income, regional and balanced-reference sensitivity is implemented. Review the July 1 classification convention and the changing reference population before selecting the final peer comparison.
5. Statistical review: inspect residuals and influential cases, cross-country dependence and uncertainty from source indices. Country-clustered intervals and the existing sensitivity specifications are a starting point.
6. Historical interpretation: add case evidence, territorial continuity checks and a clearly stated transferability argument before policy conclusions.

## Major choices for review

- Final basket definitions and whether two health measures overweight health.
- Whether income controls remove part of the institutional pathway of interest.
- Whether realized trade or tariffs answer the intended openness question.
- Whether the intervention question is feasible across the intended countries and years.
- Final analysis period, historical peers and stage-comparison rules.

Do not choose indicators because they support a preferred conclusion. Keep results that challenge initial expectations visible.
