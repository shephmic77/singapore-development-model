Historical policy measures and models

Exports are goods and services as a share of GDP. FDI is net inward investment as a share of GDP. Public capital is the IMF estimate of accumulated government assets divided by real GDP in the same constant 2017 PPP units, multiplied by 100. A stock can exceed one year of GDP.

V-Dem impartial administration provides a separate historical measure of how officials apply rules. Its 0–4 scale is distinct from World Bank government effectiveness. Source values include provider estimates. Project predictions appear only in the separate gap pilot.

Each comparison uses the equal-weight mean of reporting economies outside the 11 cases. Counts below 100 are flagged. Historical reporting units need boundary and continuity review. Singapore before 1965 is outside the independent-country analysis.
