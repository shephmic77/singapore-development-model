Historical gaps in the 11 focus economies

I checked 1965 through 1999 for each economy. Source values include estimates already published by providers. My own estimates are listed separately below.

I try a straight line only for gaps of one or two years with source values on both sides. I hide known values in blocks of the same length in the same economy and measure the typical error. I require at least ten validation points and error no greater than copying the nearest value. Validation windows can overlap, so these errors are a screening check.

I leave leading and trailing gaps open, including early government effectiveness. Survey and expert index gaps stay open in this pilot. Happiness uses published periods and is outside this annual pilot. The estimates stay separate from original data, scores, models, and benchmarks.
